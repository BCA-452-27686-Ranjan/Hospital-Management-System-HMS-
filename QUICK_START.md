# Quick Start Guide - HMS Simple Registration

## 🚀 Easy User Registration

The HMS now includes a **simple registration system** that makes it incredibly easy for users to sign up!

### 📋 Registration Process

#### **Step 1: Access Registration**
- Go to: `http://localhost:8000/`
- Click **"Get Started"** button
- Or directly access: `http://localhost:8000/accounts/simple-register/`

#### **Step 2: Choose Your Role**
- **Patient**: For patients who want to book appointments
- **Doctor**: For doctors who want to manage appointments and patients

#### **Step 3: Fill Basic Information**
- **Required Fields:**
  - First Name & Last Name
  - Username (unique)
  - Email Address
  - Phone Number
  - Password (8+ characters)
  - Confirm Password

#### **Step 4: Role-Specific Details**

**For Patients:**
- Date of Birth
- Gender
- Blood Group

**For Doctors:**
- Medical Specialization
- Medical License Number
- Years of Experience

#### **Step 5: Complete Registration**
- Accept Terms & Conditions
- Click **"Create Account"**
- **Auto-login** after successful registration!

### 🔍 Admin Verification

#### **Doctor Verification Process:**
1. **Registration**: Doctor creates account
2. **Pending Status**: Limited access until verified
3. **Admin Review**: Admin verifies credentials
4. **Full Access**: All doctor features enabled

#### **Verification Benefits:**
- Ensures only qualified doctors
- Maintains system quality
- Protects patient safety
- Builds trust in the platform
